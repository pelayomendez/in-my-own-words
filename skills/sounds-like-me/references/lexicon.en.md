While a lexicon is a stub, the honest behaviour is:

1. Apply `core-voice.md` in full. Structure, rhythm, argument moves and closing pattern all transfer across languages.
2. Derive only what safely follows from an observed habit in another language — an em-dash pattern, a sentence-opening habit, an emphasis device.
3. Say so on delivery, in one line. Do not invent verbal tics in a language you have never seen the author write. A fabricated habit is worse than a neutral sentence.
