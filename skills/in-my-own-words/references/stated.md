# Stated — what the author says about themselves

**Status: empty.** No grill has been run. See `grill.md`.

This is the second-authority layer. It holds what the author told you in an interview: who they write for, which of their own pieces they disown, what they always cut, what's deliberate rather than habit.

**The corpus outranks everything here.** When a stated preference and the observed profile disagree, the observed profile wins — except for scope and audience, which the corpus cannot see and where this file is the only evidence.

Nothing in here is a trait. Traits live in `core-voice.md` and the lexicons and come from text. This file records intent, and intent is what the profile has to be protected from.
